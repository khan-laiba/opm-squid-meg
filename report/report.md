## 1. Introduction {#introduction}

Placeholder. The study compares on-scalp optically pumped magnetometers (OPM) with the Neuromag
SQUID system, first on an adult head and then on smaller heads, and ends with simulated interictal
spikes. [@fig-arrays] shows the sensor arrays on the adult head and the detectability against
source depth: on the adult head the dense OPM array has {{ F.g2_dense_sites }} single-axis sensors and
the Neuromag system {{ F.g2_squid_channels }} channels ([S7](numbers.html) gives the source of each
number). Notation used later: D~child~ is a child's OPM-minus-Neuromag detectability in dB, and
cortical areas are given in cm^2^.
